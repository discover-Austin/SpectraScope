Privacy Policy

SpectraScope: Night Sessions is an entertainment application that simulates paranormal-style experiences using visual and audio effects. All experiences within the app are fictional and intended for entertainment purposes only.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal data. The app does not require user accounts, does not include analytics or tracking technologies, and does not perform any background data collection.

We do not collect, share, sell, or transmit personal or sensitive user data.

Permissions

Camera: Camera access is used only to render live visual environments during simulation sessions. Visual data is not recorded or transmitted.

Microphone: Microphone access is used only to drive real-time audio-reactive visual effects. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted in any form.

Local Storage

Any images manually saved by the user during a session are stored locally on the user’s device. These images are not transmitted off the device and can be deleted by the user at any time using the in-app controls.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data-sharing services.

Children’s Privacy

SpectraScope: Night Sessions is not designed to collect information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from users of any age.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the publisher using the contact information provided in the app store listing.
