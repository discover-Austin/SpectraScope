Privacy Policy

SpectraScope: Night Sessions is an entertainment application that simulates paranormal-style experiences using visual and audio effects.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no background activity.

Permissions

Camera access is used only to render live visual environments during simulation sessions. Microphone access is used only to drive reactive audio-visual effects. No audio or video is stored or transmitted.

Local Storage

Manual captures saved in the local gallery remain on your device. You can clear them at any time.

Contact

For questions, contact the publisher through the store listing.
